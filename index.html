The story

A coat of many colours. A pit in the desert. A dreamer who rose to rule Egypt.

Favoured by his father and hated by his brothers, Joseph is sold into slavery and carried off to Egypt. Through prison, dreams and famine he rises to stand beside Pharaoh — until the brothers who sold him arrive, hungry, at his door.

First staged by Boatseed in 2014 and now reborn as a musical — a joyful Christmas family experience of music, dance, humour and thought-provoking storytelling.
